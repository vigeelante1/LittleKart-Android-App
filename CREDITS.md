# Image credits

Every picture in the app is drawn for this app (DECISIONS D-94, D-96, D-99): product pictures, home tiles, category and sub-category pictures, and the home offer banners. The LittleKart logo and the login illustration are the live app's own artwork (DECISIONS D-38).
